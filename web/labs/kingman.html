Queue wait ≈ V × U × T. U = u/(1−u) explodes as the floor gets busy; V = (ca² + cs²)/2 is how uneven the work is; T is the average job time. Kingman, 1961. With several workers sharing one queue, the simulation uses Sakasegawa's multi-server form.
