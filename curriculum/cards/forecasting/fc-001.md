## Prompt
A grant programme funded about 6-7% of its full applications in the last two rounds. Your own application feels strong, and you would say 65%. Explain what the outside view and the inside view each say here, which one you start from, and what probability you would log.

## Model answer
The inside view builds the forecast from this case: the team, the story, how the draft reads. It gives 65%. The outside view asks how comparable applications turned out (the reference class): about 6-7% were funded. Start from the outside view, then adjust a few points for evidence that really separates your case, for example strong preparation: log about 10%, not 35% and not 65%. Every applicant feels strong, so the feeling carries little information.

## Grading notes
Full marks: names both views, starts from the base rate, adjusts by a few points (not tens), and gives a number near 7-12%. Saying "average the two" (about 35%) is a 2. Only defining the terms is a 1-2. Mentioning that the choice of reference class is arguable earns the top mark.
