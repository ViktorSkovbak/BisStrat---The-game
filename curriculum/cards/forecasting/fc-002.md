## Prompt
Explain in your own words what the Brier score measures and what "calibrated" means. Then score two calls: you said 90% and it happened; you said 90% and it did not. What does saying 50% on everything score?

## Model answer
The Brier score is the average squared gap between the stated probability and the outcome (1 if it happened, 0 if not); lower is better. 90% and it happened: (0.9-1)² = 0.01. 90% and it did not: (0.9-0)² = 0.81. Saying 50% on everything scores 0.25 every time. Calibrated means that your 70% calls come true about 70% of the time and your 90% calls about 90%. Because the miss is squared, a few confident misses cost far more than many small ones.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the definition, the two numbers (0.01 and 0.81) and 0.25 for a coin-flip forecaster, plus the idea that confident misses dominate. Describing calibration only as "being accurate" is a 2.
