## Prompt
Your forecast log shows that your 90% calls come true about 70% of the time. How much does this cost you, why, and what do you change? Include the weekly habit that finds this out.

## Model answer
Being about 20 points too sure removed about 70% of the edge over a coin-flip forecaster in the lab (edge 18% calibrated vs about 5%), because the Brier score squares the miss and confident misses dominate. Fix it from your own record: state 70% where your record says 70%, and trim all extremes accordingly; do not go timid, which has no edge at all. The habit: every call gets a number, a date and a yes/no outcome; resolve weekly; after about 50 resolved calls plot them in bins (said vs happened) and correct. Training, teaming and tracking improved accuracy in the forecasting tournaments (Mellers et al. 2014), so compare notes with someone.

## Grading notes
Full marks: sizes the cost (large, most of the edge), gives the squared-miss reason, fixes the number from the record rather than going timid, and names the logging-and-resolving habit with a sample size. Saying "be more humble" without a method is a 2.
