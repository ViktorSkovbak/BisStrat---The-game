## Prompt
A €60M seed fund buys 12% of your company at entry. Later rounds will cut that stake to about half. Roughly what exit value returns the whole fund from this one company, and what does it tell you about a "good" €300M exit?

## Model answer
Ownership at exit is about 12% x 50% = 6%. Exit needed = fund size / ownership at exit = €60M / 6% = about €1B. A €300M exit gives the fund about €18M, 0.3x the fund: good for the founder, not a fund-returner. The bar rises with fund size (a €200M fund needs about €3.3B) and falls with more ownership or less dilution.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the formula or the steps, the right order of magnitude (about €1B, not hundreds of millions), and the reading of €300M as about 0.3x the fund. Forgetting dilution gives €500M: a 2.
