## Prompt
A venture partner chooses between a company with a likely 3x outcome, a company with a 10% chance of 100x (about 50x to the fund after dilution) and a steady profitable business. Which one does a fund want more of, and why? Name the catch.

## Model answer
The moonshot: chance x size of the win is 10% x 50x = 5x per bet, against 75% x 3x = about 2.3x for the likely 3x and about 1.2x for the steady business (90% x 1.3x). In the lab's simulation a fund of 25 moonshots returns about 4x against about 2.3x and about 1.1x. A fund is paid for the size of its wins, not their frequency. The catch: it needs enough shots, because roughly 1 fund in 13 with 25 moonshots gets no hit at all; and the 10% must be a credible number, not a founder's hope.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the chance x size logic with rough numbers, the right pick, and a catch (enough shots, or a credibility check on the probability). Picking the safe business is a 0-1 on the call.
