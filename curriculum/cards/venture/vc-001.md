## Prompt
A seed fund of 25 investments finds that about half of them lose money. Explain why that does not mean the fund is doing badly, and what has to be true of the other half for the fund to work. Use the Horsley Bridge figures.

## Model answer
Venture payoffs are capped at the bottom (you lose what you put in) and uncapped at the top (a winner can return 50x or 100x), so a few outliers dominate the sum. In the Horsley Bridge data (about 7,000 investments, 1985-2014), about half returned less than the money put in, while the 6% that returned 10x or more produced about 60% of total returns. The fund works if a few investments are very large wins, not if most are small ones. Judge the portfolio, not the individual loss.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the asymmetry (capped loss, uncapped win), the two numbers (about half lose; about 6% make about 60%) and the conclusion about reading the portfolio. Saying only "VC is risky" or "winners pay for losers" with no numbers is a 2-3.
