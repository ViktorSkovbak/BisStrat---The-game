## Prompt
You have six weeks before a big negotiation and time for one lever: a second customer in talks, a stronger value case, or a lower opening price to look reasonable. Rank them and explain why.

## Model answer
First, the second customer. It raises your floor, narrows the zone from below and lowers the cost of a failed deal, so it shifts the whole outcome; in the lab model it added about €35 a unit (+4%). Second, the value case: it lifts what the customer will pay, but its effect is uncertain and smaller (about €15). Last, a lower opening price: it hands the customer a lower anchor and cost about €35. Rule: your power in the room comes from what you can do if you walk out, so prepare the alternative first, and be honest about it.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the ranking, the mechanism (floor and walk-away power) and the caveat that the alternative must be real. Ranking arguments above alternatives without reasoning is a 1-2. Saying the lower price "builds goodwill" without noting the anchor cost is a 2.
