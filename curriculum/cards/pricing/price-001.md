## Prompt
Your investors ask where the next profit will come from, and your team proposes selling 5% more units. Explain why a 5% price rise usually adds more profit than 5% more volume, and name the order of the four levers (price, variable cost, volume, fixed cost).
## Model answer
A euro of price goes straight to profit, while an extra unit brings only its margin (for example €800 on a €2,000 unit at a 40% margin). In McKinsey's study of about 2,400 companies, 1% on price (volume unchanged) lifted operating profit about 11%, variable cost about 8%, volume about 3%, fixed cost about 2%. The order is price, variable cost, volume, fixed cost. The catch: it assumes few customers leave.
## Grading notes
Full marks need the mechanism (price falls to profit, volume only brings margin), the order of the levers, and the caveat that the result assumes volume holds. Saying only "price is more powerful" is a 1-2.
