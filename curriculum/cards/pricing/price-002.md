## Prompt
A customer asks for 10% off. Your margin is 40%. Roughly how much more volume do you need just to earn the same profit, and what changes if your margin is 20%? Say why the answer is so large.
## Model answer
Extra units needed = discount ÷ (margin − discount). At a 40% margin, 10% off needs about 33% more units (10 ÷ 30). At a 20% margin, 10% off needs 100% more (10 ÷ 10). The reason: the discount comes straight off the margin you keep on every unit, so you must sell many more units, each earning less, to get back to the same total.
## Grading notes
Full marks need roughly 33% and roughly 100%, and the mechanism (the discount cuts the margin on every unit). Giving "10%" is a 1. Exact arithmetic is not required; the order of magnitude and the effect of a thinner margin are.
