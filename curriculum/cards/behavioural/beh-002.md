## Prompt
A pilot customer has used your product free for three months and saves about €3,000 a year. You will charge €2,000 a year. Which wording would you test first, and why might a "50% off the list price" offer work less well than you expect?

## Model answer
Word it from where the customer already stands: they have the saving, so stopping loses €3,000 a year, and that loss weighs about 2x the €2,000 price. A discount tries to move the reference to a list price this customer has never paid, so it adds little; it works better for first-time buyers with no reference yet. Keep the loss frame honest and test it on real pilots, since real framing effects are smaller than a model suggests.

## Grading notes
Needs the reference-point mechanism and the contrast between users and new buyers. Picking the loss frame without the mechanism is a 2. Naming a limit (trust, B2B buyers, small real effects) lifts it to 5.
