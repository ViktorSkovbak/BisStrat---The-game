title: A price rise is a loss

## Prompt
You need about 10% more revenue from 100 paying customers. Name two ways to get there and say which creates fewer losses in customers' minds, and why. Does splitting a 10% rise into two 5% steps help?

## Model answer
A price rise is felt as a loss from today's price, so the aim is to avoid creating a loss: add a premium tier that customers can step up to (a gain), and leave the old price in place. Splitting a rise into two steps helps little, because each step is a fresh loss from a reset reference price; in the lab it was no better than one rise. Caveat: a tier that strips features from the base plan becomes a loss again.

## Grading notes
Full marks: the premium-tier (or choice) route, the reason (loss from the old price), and that slicing does not help much. Choosing "two small rises" because "smaller feels gentler" is a 1-2.
