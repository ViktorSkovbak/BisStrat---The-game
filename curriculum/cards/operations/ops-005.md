## Prompt
A pilot customer wants a guaranteed two-week turnaround on change requests. Your team runs at 95%. What is the cheapest credible commitment, and what do you change?

## Model answer
At 95% the queue alone makes two weeks unreliable. Reserve capacity: a priority class or a small fixed slice of the team for pilot requests, so their effective load is around 70%, plus a WIP limit on the rest. Promise the turnaround only for requests within a defined size. The real decision is accepting that the core build slows a little; slack is what you buy.

## Grading notes
A 5 names reserved capacity or a priority class, bounds the request size, and states the trade-off with the core build.
