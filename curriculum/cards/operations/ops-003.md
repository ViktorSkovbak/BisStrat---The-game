## Prompt
Name two ways to cut waiting time that add no capacity, and the term of the VUT equation each one hits.

## Model answer
Cut arrival variability (ca): smooth intake, schedule work, batch requests into a regular cadence. Cut service variability (cs): standard work, smaller and clearer tasks, fewer interruptions. Both shrink V = (ca² + cs²)/2. Limiting WIP also works, via Little's law.

## Grading notes
Two concrete levers mapped to V (or to WIP via Little) = 5. Generic "be more efficient" = 1.
