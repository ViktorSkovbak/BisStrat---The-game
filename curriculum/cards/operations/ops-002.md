## Prompt
Your engineering board shows 40 open items. The team finishes 10 a week. What is the average lead time, and how do you halve it without anyone working faster?

## Model answer
L = λW, so W = L/λ = 40/10 = 4 weeks. Halve the work in progress to 20 items (stop starting, finish first, WIP limits) and lead time halves to 2 weeks at the same throughput.

## Grading notes
Needs the 4 weeks and the WIP lever. "Hire more people" without the WIP point is a 2.
