## Prompt
Your team runs at 80% utilisation. You add work until it runs at 95%. Roughly how much longer do queued jobs now wait, and why is the effect so large?

## Model answer
Wait scales with u/(1-u): 4 at 80%, 19 at 95%, so about 4.75x longer (6x at 96%). Idle time cannot be banked, so at high load every burst leaves a queue residue that has no slack to drain.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the ratio (roughly 5x) and the mechanism (bursts plus no slack). Saying only "a bit longer" or "proportionally longer" is a 1-2.
