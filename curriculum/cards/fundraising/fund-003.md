## Prompt
You need €3M for your pilot milestone. You can sell 25% of the company for €3M, or sell 14% for €1.5M and win a €2.5M grant. Which would you try for, what could go wrong, and what do you do about it?

## Model answer
Try for the grant route: it sells 14% instead of 25% and leaves about €4M, enough for the milestone plus a buffer, so the Series A is priced from strength. In the lab the founders' money at exit was about 22% higher. The risks are time and certainty: grants take months, may not be awarded and bring reporting and co-funding. If the grant fails you are short of the milestone and must bridge at a discount (the lab's worst case). So start the application early, keep an investor fallback, and do not plan to spend the grant money before it is awarded.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the dilution saving, the buffer logic and a concrete fallback. Calling the grant "free money" with no risk is a 1-2. Naming a specific programme is a plus only if the facts are right (the EIC Accelerator's grant is below €2.5M and aimed at TRL 6-8).
