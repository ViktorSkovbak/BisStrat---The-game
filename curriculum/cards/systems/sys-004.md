## Prompt
Your purchasing manager reorders based on the last two weeks of orders. How would you change the forecasting rule, roughly how much calmer would your orders become, and what is the danger of smoothing too far?

## Model answer
React to the trend, not the week: average over a quarter or so (several times the total delay) instead of two weeks. In the model this cut the swing from 20× to 7× (about 3× calmer); a month's average gave 2×; chasing last week's number was worse (0.7×). Beyond about a quarter the gain flattens (a half-year average, 6×), and you become slow to see a real, lasting change in demand, so you must reset the baseline when sell-through confirms it.

## Grading notes
Full marks: longer averaging window, a rough size (about 3×), the diminishing returns, and the risk of missing a real shift. Saying only "forecast better" is a 1.
