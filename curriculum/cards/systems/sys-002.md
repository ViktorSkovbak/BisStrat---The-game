## Prompt
Your orders to a component supplier swing wildly. A colleague suggests more safety stock. Which single change would you try first instead, and what does extra safety stock do to the swing?

## Model answer
Share real end-customer sell-through so every step plans from the same demand. In the model this cut the swing about 5× (10× to 2×); halving shipping times about 1.4×; smaller lots about 1.1×. Extra safety stock made it worse (10× to 15×) because each step must also build up the cushion when the forecast rises, and it ties up cash. Stock covers noise, not the cause.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the lever (information sharing), a rough size (several times, not marginal), and why safety stock does not help. Saying "more stock" or "shorter lead time" without the information point is 1-2.
