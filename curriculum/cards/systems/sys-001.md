## Prompt
Consumers raise their buying of your product by 10%. A few weeks later your component supplier is being asked for orders that swing several times that. Explain what law is at work, name two or three of its causes, and say why sensible managers still produce it.

## Model answer
The bullwhip effect: orders become more volatile than sales, and the swing grows with each step upstream. Causes: forecasting from the next step's orders rather than from consumer sales (a small rise becomes a larger forecast), delays between ordering and receiving, order lots, price promotions and shortage gaming. Each step's reaction is reasonable from its own view, but together they amplify; the structure creates it, not stupidity.

## Grading notes
Full marks: the law, at least two causes with the mechanism (forecasting from orders, delays or lots), and the point that each step amplifies. Naming only "poor communication" is a 1-2.
