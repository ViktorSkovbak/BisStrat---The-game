## Prompt
You want to stay CEO of your company after Series A. One lead investor offers a balanced board with you as CEO and chair. Another deal keeps a founder board majority but you would hire an outside CEO. Which do you take, and why?

## Model answer
Take the balanced board with you as CEO and chair. The CEO job lives at the board's pleasure, so a balanced board gives better-than-even odds of still running the company, and compared with holding the majority too it lifts exit euros by roughly a quarter (one control fewer). The majority with an outside CEO pays the price of control (about a fifth of value per control) without giving you the one thing a king wants, the CEO chair. Buy the cheapest control that protects what you want, not all of it.

## Grading notes
Needs the insight that the CEO job depends on the board, and that control must protect the thing you care about. Choosing the majority "because control is safer" is a 1-2. Credit sizing: roughly 20% of value per control.
