## Prompt
Explain "rich or king". Then say how the right move differs between the two goals when a lead investor asks for board seats at your Series A.

## Model answer
Founders must choose between maximising the value of the company and accepting less control (rich), and staying in charge while accepting a smaller company or a smaller euro outcome (king). Rich: accept the investor-led board and bring in experienced people early, since resources arrive with conditions. King: keep the CEO job and enough board strength (a balanced board with you in the chair) to protect it. The costly mistake is to want both without seeing the trade-off, and to learn it from a term sheet.

## Grading notes
Needs both definitions and a different action for each goal. Naming only one goal, or saying "keep some control" without a reason, is a 2. A 5 states that the choice should be made before the term sheet.
