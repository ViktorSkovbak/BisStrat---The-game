## Prompt
In your own words: what did Wasserman find about founders who keep control of the board and the CEO job, and roughly how big is the effect for a company where you would keep all three controls (CEO, chair, board majority)?

## Model answer
Startups where the founder still holds the CEO job and board control tend to be less valuable: in a sample of 6,130 US startups, each extra level of founder control was associated with a 17-22% lower pre-money valuation. Three controls compound to about half the value (0.8 x 0.8 x 0.8 = 0.5). It is an association, not proof of cause, and it shows most for companies older than about three years.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the direction (control goes with lower value), the size (about a fifth per control, about half for three) and a hedge (association). Saying only "control hurts" is a 2; a wrong magnitude (for example 2% or 80%) is a 1.
