## Prompt
In the lab, the same weekly split of a founder's 55 hours lowered output per person by about 9% when the team grew from 12 to 30. Explain why, say what the founder should change first, and name what limits the fix.

## Model answer
The hours people need from the founder (direction, specs, 1:1s, unblocking) grow with headcount; the founder's hours do not. The same 10 hours of 1:1s covers about 58% of what 12 people need but about 32% of what 30 need, so each person is more often stuck or unclear. First change: move own engineering or sales hours into priorities, specs and 1:1s (about +8% on what the week adds in the model). Hiring faster makes it worse: it dilutes time again. The limit is span of control: past roughly 8 direct reports you need trained leads, who also have leverage and cost coaching time. (Illustrative model, not measured.)

## Grading notes
Full marks need the mechanism (needs scale with headcount, hours do not), the right first move (own work into direction and 1:1s), and the span limit or leads. Answering "hire more managers" without the cost of training them is a 2-3. Recommending faster hiring as the main fix is wrong.
