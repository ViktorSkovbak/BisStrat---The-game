## Prompt
A founder-CEO says: "I produce more than anyone on the team, so I am doing my job." Using Grove's definition of a manager's output, explain why this can be wrong and what the founder should measure instead.

## Model answer
Grove defines a manager's output as the output of the organisation they lead, plus that of neighbouring teams they influence; it is not the work they personally produce. A founder who writes the most code can still lower output if ten people wait on priorities or blockers. The measure is what the team produces, and each activity is judged by its leverage: the output it generates per hour. Own work counts, but only as one activity among several.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the definition in own words (team output plus influenced teams, not personal output), the idea of leverage per activity, and one concrete example of a founder's hour that raises or lowers others' output. Saying "managers should delegate" without the leverage logic is a 2.
