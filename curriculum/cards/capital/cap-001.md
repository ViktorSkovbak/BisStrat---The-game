## Prompt
A product line earns 8% on its capital and your investors expect 10%. The board wants to double its growth. Explain what happens to the line's value and why, and say what you would do first.

## Model answer
Value falls. Growth adds value only when each new euro earns more than it costs, and at 8% against 10% every euro invested is worth less than a euro. Doubling growth means reinvesting more of the profit, so less can be taken out. In the lab, a line at 8% growing 4% is worth about 67; growing 8% it is worth nothing. Fix the return first (the lab's 8% to 14% is worth 2.5x), and only then grow.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the direction (value falls), the mechanism (new money earns less than it costs, more of the profit is reinvested) and the action (fix the return first). "Growth is risky" or "growth needs cash" without the return-versus-cost link is a 1-2.
