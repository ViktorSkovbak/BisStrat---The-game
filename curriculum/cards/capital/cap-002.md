## Prompt
You have spare cash and two lines: A earns 25% and grows slowly; B earns 8% and grows fast; capital costs 10%. Rank "put it in B", "put it in A" and "pay it out", and explain the rule.

## Model answer
A first, pay-out second, B last. A euro in A is worth about 2.9 euros of value (the lab's 25% line), a euro paid out is worth 1, a euro in B is worth about 0.7. Rank uses of cash by the gap between return and cost of capital, not by growth; if nothing clears the cost of capital, return the cash. Catch: the return on the next euro in A falls as A saturates, and A stops beating a pay-out when the return on the new money reaches the cost of capital.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the order, the "gap, not growth" rule, and the saturation catch. Picking B because it is growing fastest is the instinct this card exists to break (grade 1).
