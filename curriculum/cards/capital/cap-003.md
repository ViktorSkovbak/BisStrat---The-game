## Prompt
Write the value-driver formula in words, say what g/ROIC means, and say what the formula shows when ROIC equals the cost of capital.

## Model answer
Value = next year's after-tax operating profit x (1 - growth / ROIC) / (cost of capital - growth). Growth / ROIC is the share of profit that must be reinvested to grow at that rate: the higher the return, the less you reinvest per point of growth. When ROIC equals the cost of capital, the growth terms cancel and value is just profit divided by the cost of capital, whatever the growth. Growth is then worth nothing: in the lab, a line at 10% is worth 100 whether it grows 0%, 4% or 8%.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the reinvestment reading of g/ROIC and the cancellation at ROIC = WACC, in own words. The algebra is not required; reciting the formula without the meaning is a 2.
