## Prompt
When is promoting the best performer the right rule, and what in the role tells you? Give an example from an engineering company.

## Model answer
When the new job is mostly the old craft, so skill transfers (the common-sense world). Example: a tech lead who codes about 70% of the time and mainly makes technical judgements. In the lab best engineers gave 20.1 of 20 against 19.6 for a trial and 18.8 for random. Check: what share of the new role's time and success depends on the old skill? The higher, the more you can promote on current performance.

## Grading notes
Needs the transfer condition and a concrete role. "Always promote the best" or "never promote the best" = 1-2.
