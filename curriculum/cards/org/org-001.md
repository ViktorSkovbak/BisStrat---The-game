## Prompt
Explain the Peter principle in your own words and say when promoting your best performer lowers output. What single question tells you which case you are in?

## Model answer
People are promoted for how well they do the current job, and the new job needs a different skill. If skill in the new job is only weakly related to skill in the old one, the promoted star is on average only average at the new job, and you lose their old output too. Ask: how much does performance in the old job predict performance in the new one? Weak link: best-first lowers output (Pluchino et al. simulation: -10% vs random about +1%). Strong link: best-first is right (+9%).

## Grading notes
Full marks need the mechanism (performance now vs different skill later) and the transfer question. "Smart people get promoted too far" without the mechanism is a 1-2.
