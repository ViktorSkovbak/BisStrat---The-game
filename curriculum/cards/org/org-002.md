## Prompt
You must choose 4 team leads from 20 engineers, and engineering skill barely predicts managing skill. Rank three rules (best engineers, random, a management trial) and say roughly how much better the winner is.

## Model answer
Management trial first, random second, best engineers last. In the lab the trial lost about 2.7 times less output than promoting the best engineers (random about 1.4 times less), because even a noisy read of managing skill beats none, and best-first also pulls your top coders off the keyboard. A trial means acting lead on a real project plus feedback from the team.

## Grading notes
Correct ranking with the reason (trial reads the new skill; best-first removes top output) = 5. Right ranking, no mechanism = 3. Best engineers first = 1.
