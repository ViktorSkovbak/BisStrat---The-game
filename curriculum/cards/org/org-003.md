## Prompt
Your strongest engineer expects to be made a lead, but you do not think she would be a good manager. What do you offer her, what does it cost you if you do nothing, and what makes the offer fail?

## Model answer
Offer a technical track (principal engineer) with the same pay, title and a real say in architecture and hiring. Promoting her costs twice: you lose most of her output (in the lab the top four kept only 1.7 of 5.7 units) and get a weak manager; doing nothing risks a slump or her leaving (the lab assumes 8%, not a measured figure). The offer fails when the technical title is a consolation prize with less pay or status than management.

## Grading notes
Needs the ladder, the double cost, and the failure condition (parity of pay and status). Just "give her a raise" = 2.
