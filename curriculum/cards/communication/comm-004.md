## Prompt
Your all-hands went well and nobody had questions. How do you find out whether the strategy landed, which group will be furthest from the truth, and why is sending the long strategy document the weakest next step?

## Model answer
Silence and "any questions?" prove nothing: you judge by what you would understand. Instead give people live cases to decide with the strategy (for example, "should we take this custom request from a non-pilot customer?") and see what they choose, weekly. Check first the people furthest from your background, such as sales or new hires: the gap there is largest (in the lab's model about 2.8 times larger than for a typical engineer, and even the closest colleagues keep little after one telling). Live cases also repeat the message and train use. A long document is mostly unread and adds almost nothing to recall (about 1.1x in the model), though it is fine as reference.

## Grading notes
Needs a decision-based test (not a survey of feelings), the furthest group named, and the reason the document fails (no retrieval, no use). Suggesting only "ask them to explain it back" earns a 3: better than silence, but it does not test application.
