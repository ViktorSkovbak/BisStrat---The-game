## Prompt
You must get 15 engineers and 3 sales people to make daily decisions that fit this year's strategy. You have a thorough 40-slide deck. Do you present it? What do you give them instead, and roughly how much better does it work after four weeks?

## Model answer
Present the deck only as reference. The working message is one sentence a new hire could repeat, plus a real example of a decision it settles (for instance "we only build what helps win or keep our three pilots; so we decline the custom feature for a non-pilot client"). In the lab's illustrative model, one such sentence told weekly in two channels leaves about 60% able to state and apply it after four weeks, against about 2% for the deck told once: more than 20 times better. Concreteness and repetition multiply; detail does not stick for people without the context.

## Grading notes
Needs: a one-sentence core, a real example, and a clear statement that detail lives elsewhere. A strong answer sizes the gap as "many times better" and says it is model-based. "Make the deck shorter" alone is a 2.
