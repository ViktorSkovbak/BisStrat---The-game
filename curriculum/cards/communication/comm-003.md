## Prompt
A founder says "I told everyone the strategy at the all-hands; I do not want to sound like a broken record." Rebut this using the forgetting curve and Kotter's rule of thumb, and say what a good repetition policy looks like for a team of 18 over a month.

## Model answer
Memory drops steeply after one exposure (Ebbinghaus; replicated by Murre and Dros, 2015), so after a month a once-told message is mostly gone. Each spaced repeat makes the memory last longer, and each channel reaches people the last one missed. Kotter's rule of thumb is that leaders undercommunicate a vision by a factor of 10 or more (not a measured figure). A policy: the same sentence weekly for at least four weeks, in at least two channels (all-hands and team chat, plus 1:1s), with a new real example each time, then monthly after that.

## Grading notes
Needs the forgetting mechanism, the spacing or repetition effect, and a concrete cadence with channels. Quoting Kotter as an exact statistic is a mistake (it is a rule of thumb). "Repeat it more" with no cadence is a 2.
