## Prompt
You have just presented next year's strategy to your team and it felt clear. Explain in your own words why that feeling is a poor guide to how well it landed, and give the one study that shows how big the gap can be.

## Model answer
Once you know something you cannot easily imagine not knowing it (the curse of knowledge, Camerer, Loewenstein and Weber, 1989). You hear the whole context behind every sentence; the team hears only the sentence. In Newton's 1990 tapping study (via Heath and Heath), tappers expected listeners to name about half the songs, and listeners got about 1 in 40 (roughly 3 of 120; secondary sources). So expect your estimate of understanding to be many times too high, not a little high.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the mechanism (own knowledge fills in the gaps for you), the direction (you overestimate) and the order of magnitude (10x or more, not 20%). "People forget" without the overestimation point is a 2. Exact tapping numbers are a bonus, not required.
