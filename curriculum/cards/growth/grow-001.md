## Prompt
Explain in your own words why sales of a new B2B product start slowly, accelerate, peak and then fall, even if marketing spend never changes. Name the two sources of buyers in the Bass model and say roughly when the peak comes.

## Model answer
Buyers come from innovators (p: your own marketing and curiosity, a steady trickle that does not depend on others) and imitators (q: word of mouth, proportional to how many have already bought). At launch only p works, so sales are slow. As customers accumulate, word of mouth takes over and sales accelerate. Late on, few untouched buyers remain, so sales fall. Cumulative adoption is an S-curve; yearly sales are a bell that peaks when roughly (q - p) / 2q of the market has bought, about 47% at p = 0.02 and q = 0.40, so a little before half the market.

## Grading notes
Full marks need both engines named with their different dependence on past buyers, and the peak near (a bit before) half the market. Saying only "S-curve" or "adoption spreads" is a 1-2.
