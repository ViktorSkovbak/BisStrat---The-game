## Prompt
You have 300k euros and two options, each worth +40% on its own lever: more marketing, or a referral programme with referenceable pilots. About 3,000 buyers exist and p is roughly 0.02, q roughly 0.40. Which wins by year 5 and how big is the gap? When does the other option lead, and why?

## Model answer
Referral (q) wins: year-5 sales about 395 a year against about 300 for marketing and 247 today (roughly 1.6x against 1.2x). Marketing leads for the first three years; word of mouth overtakes during year 4. The same percentage is applied to numbers about 20 times apart: once even 5% of buyers have bought, q times the share bought already exceeds p, and by year 5 about four in five new customers come through word of mouth. The catch: it only works if customers are happy, because word of mouth multiplies what they say, and in the first year there is nobody to talk.

## Grading notes
Needs the right winner, a size (about 1.6x vs about 1.2x, or "clearly bigger"), the early-lead crossover and the reason (q far larger than p). Picking marketing, or saying "it depends" with no mechanism, is a 1-2.
