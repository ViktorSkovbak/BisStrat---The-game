## Prompt
Your hardware company shows a profit every month and orders are growing 10% a month, yet the bank balance is falling. Explain in your own words how that is possible, and name the three terms that make up the cash conversion cycle.

## Model answer
Profit counts a sale when it is made; cash arrives only when the customer pays, and stock and parts are paid for before the sale. Growth means each month you must fund more stock and more unpaid invoices than the month before, so cash tied up grows faster than profit comes in. The cycle is days of inventory + days of receivables − days of payables: the days your money is out of the bank between paying for parts and being paid.

## Grading notes
Full marks need the mechanism (cash goes out before it comes in, and growth makes the gap bigger each month) and all three terms with the right signs. Saying only "costs are high" or "customers pay late" is a 1-2.
