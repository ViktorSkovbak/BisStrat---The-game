## Prompt
Your cash plan shows the balance bottoming at −€1.05M in month 18 if nothing slips. How much do you raise, when do you start, and why is a bigger customer order not always good news at this point?

## Model answer
Raise the peak need plus a third to a half (about €1.4-1.6M), because a faster ramp or slower payers moves the need quickly: one extra point of monthly growth added about €0.35M in the lab. Start 6-9 months before the dip, since investors will see an empty bank at the worst moment. A big order on today's terms makes the hole grow faster than the growth (15% needed 3.4× the money of 10%), so take it only with deposits, better supplier terms or signed funding.

## Grading notes
Full marks need a size above the peak (not equal to it), a reason for the cushion, early timing, and the point that faster growth on slow terms deepens the hole. Answering "exactly €1.05M" or "as much as possible" is a 2.
